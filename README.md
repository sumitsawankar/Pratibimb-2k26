# Pratibimb 2K26 – Annual College Cultural Fest Website

Pratibimb 2K26 is the official event website developed for our college's annual cultural festival. The platform serves as a centralized hub where students, participants, sponsors, and visitors can explore event details, competitions, schedules, registrations, announcements, and festival highlights.

Designed with a modern and engaging interface, the website reflects the energy and creativity of the festival while providing a seamless user experience across all devices.

---

## Live Demo

https://pratibimb-2k26.vercel.app/

---

# Overview

Pratibimb 2K26 celebrates talent, creativity, innovation, and cultural diversity through a variety of competitions, performances, workshops, and entertainment events.

This website was created to simplify event promotion and provide participants with all the necessary information in one place. It offers an intuitive interface for browsing events, understanding schedules, and connecting with organizers.

# Website Sections

## Home

An engaging hero section introducing Pratibimb 2K26 with festival branding, event highlights, and call-to-action buttons.

---

## About

Provides an overview of Pratibimb, its vision, objectives, and the significance of the annual cultural festival.

---

## Events

Displays all competitions and activities organized during the fest.

Examples include:

- Cultural Events
- Dance Competitions
- Singing Competitions
- Technical Events
- Gaming Events
- Photography
- Fashion Show
- Drama
- Literary Events
- Fun Activities

Each event includes:

- Event Description
- Rules & Guidelines
- Date & Time
- Venue
- Registration Details

---

## Schedule

A structured timeline of the festival including:

- Opening Ceremony
- Competitions
- Workshops
- Guest Sessions
- Cultural Performances
- Prize Distribution
- Closing Ceremony

---

## Gallery

A showcase of previous event highlights, memorable moments, and promotional visuals.

---

## Sponsors

Displays supporting organizations, sponsors, and event partners.

---

## Organizing Committee

Introduces the event coordinators, volunteers, faculty coordinators, and organizing team responsible for managing the festival.

Provides contact information for:
